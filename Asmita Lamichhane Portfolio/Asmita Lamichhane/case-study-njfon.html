Client Project · Community Website

NJFON

NJFON (Neymar Jr Fans of Nepal) is a community website created to bring fan content and news into one responsive online experience. This was my second client project.

Project typeSecond client project
FocusCommunity website
StackHTML · CSS · JavaScript
LocationNepal

The goal

Create an online home for Neymar Jr supporters in Nepal, with clear navigation between community information, news, events, committee details and gallery content.

My contribution

I worked on the website’s structure and front-end presentation, organizing content into clear sections and shaping a responsive interface for fan content and updates.

Design considerations

The main priorities were consistent navigation, readable content, responsive layouts and a recognizable fan-community identity. The interface needed to feel energetic while keeping information easy to find.

What I learned

As my second client project, NJFON gave me another opportunity to work with a real project brief and consider how design and development decisions support a community’s needs.

Next steps

Further improvements could include collecting feedback from community members, measuring which sections visitors use most and documenting accessibility checks across devices.
