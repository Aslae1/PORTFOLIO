The Last Glitch

The Last Glitch was my first global hackathon participation: a team project framed as an interactive cyberpunk story about digital immortality and the risk of losing emotional humanity.

RoleFirst global hackathon participation · Team project
FormatGlobal hackathon team project
StackJavaScript · HTML · CSS
ThemeIdentity · Memory · Emotion

The central idea

The story asks: if a person’s digital existence can continue indefinitely, what happens to emotion, identity and the qualities that make someone human? The cyberpunk setting gives that question a visual and narrative frame.

Experience goals

Rather than presenting the idea as a static essay, the project uses a web-based story format intended to invite exploration and let readers engage with the narrative at their own pace.

Design direction

The visual direction draws on cyberpunk atmosphere and digital-glitch language to reinforce the story’s themes. Visual effects should support readability and narrative focus rather than compete with the text.

What I learned

This project brings together interface decisions and storytelling. It encourages me to think about navigation, pacing and visual feedback as part of the narrative itself.

Next steps

Future refinements could include testing the reading flow on mobile, improving keyboard accessibility and checking that effects remain comfortable for users who prefer reduced motion.
