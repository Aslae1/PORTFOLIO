AIRWAY

AIRWAY is an air-writing recognition prototype. You write a character in the air, the camera follows your hand, and the system turns what you wrote into digital text.

TypeComputer vision prototype
InputHand gestures through a camera
ToolsOpenCV · MediaPipe
OutputRecognized text

The problem

Typing and drawing both need a device you touch. This prototype explores a hands-free alternative where a gesture in front of a camera becomes a character on screen.

Approach

Hand tracking follows the fingertip across frames, the path is collected as a stroke, and a recognition step maps that stroke to a character. The recognized output is then converted into digital text.

What I learned

Working with live camera input showed me how lighting, hand speed and jitter affect recognition, and why smoothing and clear start and stop gestures matter as much as the model itself.

Next steps

Test with more writers and lighting conditions, add a confidence indicator, and build a simple browser interface so anyone can try it.
